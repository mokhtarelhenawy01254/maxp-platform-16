# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Arabic-speaking merchants, marketers, and operations teams inside MaxP's multi-tenant SaaS. They manage product content, campaigns, and storefronts. They need quick, on-brand social-media visuals without writing raw prompts.

## Product Purpose

A structured AI image generator that turns product/campaign data into platform-ready social-media images. It hides prompt engineering behind familiar form fields (product info, brand colors, copy, style) and outputs an image the user can download, copy, or regenerate.

## Brand Personality

Expert, calm, Arabic-first, business-credible. The interface should feel like a capable assistant, not a toy. Confidence without noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI tool dashboards overloaded with neon gradients and "magic" copy.
- Long, undifferentiated form dumps.
- Western-first UI patterns with Arabic bolted on.
- Modals used where inline progressive disclosure would do.

## Design Principles

1. **Progressive disclosure**: show the fields that matter for the current step; advanced options stay reachable, never in the way.
2. **Right-to-left as default**: every label, action, and feedback reads naturally in Arabic; English terms are secondary and localized.
3. **Preview-first**: the user should always understand what will be generated before pressing the button.
4. **Confidence through constraint**: fewer, clearer choices beat exhaustive option lists.
5. **Feedback without interruption**: status and errors appear inline and dismissible, not as blocking modals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA baseline.
- Keyboard-navigable tabs, selects, and color pickers.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Arabic screen-reader compatible labels and aria-live status alerts.
