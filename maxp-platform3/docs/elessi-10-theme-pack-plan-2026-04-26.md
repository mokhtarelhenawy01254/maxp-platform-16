# Elessi 10-Theme Pack Plan - 2026-04-26

## Scope

- anchor reference: `Elessi`
- pack mode:
  - `reference-locked expansion pack`
- target:
  - إطلاق `10` قوالب جديدة
  - كلها من نفس روح `Elessi`
  - كلها مختلفة إدراكيًا عن بعضها
  - وكلها تضيف للمكتبة الحالية بدل أن تعتمد على واجهاتها الموجودة

## Pack Promise

- هذا الـ pack ليس `10 skins`
- هذا الـ pack هو:
  - `Elessi DNA`
  - + `10 category-specific themes`
  - + `new shared capabilities`
  - + `new user-facing families`

## Shared Elessi DNA For The Pack

- rich catalog commerce
- merchandising-first sections
- quick interactions
- dense but scannable shop surfaces
- PDP decision surfaces with trust + service + add-to-cart proximity
- mobile utility, not desktop shrinkage

## Shared Capability Wave To Build First

- `ajax-search-suggestions`
- `off-canvas-cart`
- `off-canvas-wishlist`
- `quick-view`
- `compare-entry`
- `swatch-system`
- `sticky-buy-bar`
- `bought-together`
- `trusted-badges`
- `delivery-return-rail`

## Pack-Wide Rules

- all visible critical families are `New to library`
- no visible critical family may come from existing themes in the current library
- shared capabilities may reuse engine logic, but visible treatment must be new
- every theme must show traceable extraction from `Elessi`
- no theme may rely on `Elessi Classic` as its skeleton
- every pair of shipped themes must differ across at least `5/8` critical visible axes:
  - hero logic
  - collections / category-entry logic
  - product-grid anatomy
  - shop chrome
  - PDP gallery framing
  - PDP decision surface
  - related / upsell surface
  - review / trust mechanism
