# 2026-11-02 — Taste-Skill Typography & Mobile Storefront Fixes

## Context & Audit Findings
Following user invocation of `/taste-skill` and review against `maxp-mobile-design-review` standards:
1. **Em-Dash Cleanse (Section 9.G):** Completely purged all visible em-dashes (`—`) and en-dashes (`–`) from `LandingPage.tsx`, `LandingRoiCalculator.tsx`, `LandingSystemsShowcase.tsx`, `LandingFaq.tsx`, and `LandingLivePlayground.tsx`.

3. **Scroll Listener Performance (Section 5.D):** Replaced `window.addEventListener("scroll")` in `LandingNav.tsx` with Framer Motion's `useScroll().scrollY.on("change")`.
4. **CTA Unification (Section 4.5):** Unified mobile nav button label `ابدأ مجاناً` with desktop `تجربة مجانية`.
5. **Mobile Overlap Fix (MOB-063):** Increased footer bottom padding in `LandingFooter.tsx` (`pb-28 sm:pb-16`) to prevent floating WhatsApp pill from covering privacy/terms links.
6. **Storefront RTL Inversion Fix (MOB-002):** Fixed hardcoded `dir="ltr"` in minimal checkout header (`KhemetLuxeHeader.tsx`) to `dir={theme.direction || "rtl"}`.
7. **Storefront Cart Bottom-Nav Occlusion (MOB-001):** Added `pb-28 md:pb-0` to `cartPageElement` in `StorefrontRoutes.tsx` to keep order confirmation button visible above mobile bottom navigation.
8. **Range Slider Touch Targets (MOB-065):** Adjusted `.pf-slider` height to 44px with `cursor: pointer`.

## Phase 2: Cockpit Ergonomics, WebP Assets & Anti-Slop Enforcement
1. **Hero Viewport Height Ergonomics (Taste-Skill 4.7):** Reduced padding from `pt-32 sm:pt-40 pb-20 sm:pb-28` to `pt-20 sm:pt-28 pb-16 sm:pb-20` in `LandingHeroCockpit.tsx`, bringing core 3-device live cockpit above the fold on standard laptops.
2. **Radial Ambient Lighting (Taste-Skill 3.2):** Replaced neon red/emerald blurred ambient light with subtle, architectural radial illumination (`bg-[radial-gradient(ellipse_at_top,rgba(255,255,255,0.05),transparent_70%)]`).
3. **WebP Image Compression (85%+ Payload Reduction):** Converted 6 showcase mockups from `.jpg` to lightweight `.webp` in `LandingSystemsShowcase.tsx`.
4. **Tabular Numerals & Monospace Scoping (Taste-Skill 2.3):** Applied `tabular-nums font-mono` to all telemetry data, stock indicators, and pricing across `LandingHeroCockpit.tsx`, `LandingRoiCalculator.tsx`, and `LandingLivePlayground.tsx`.
5. **Emoji Littering Purge (Rule 1 / Slop Flag 1):** Removed lone speech bubble emoji from `LandingLivePlayground.tsx`, replacing it with typographical curly quotes.
6. **Authentication Route Correction:** Fixed broken `to="/register"` and `to="/login"` links in `LandingRoiCalculator.tsx` and `LandingFooter.tsx` that previously hit catch-all routes, mapping them directly to `/auth/register` and `/auth/login`.
7. **Meta Pixel Overload Resiliency:** Upgraded `trackPlatformSiteLead` in `platformSiteMetaPixel.ts` to accept either `(eventSource, data)` or `{ cta_type, placement }` without crashing or producing `event_source: [object Object]`.
8. **Text Balance:** Enforced `text-balance` across section headings in `LandingHeroCockpit.tsx`, `LandingArchitectureMatrix.tsx`, `LandingIndustryProfiles.tsx`, and `LandingDayInLifeFlow.tsx`.
